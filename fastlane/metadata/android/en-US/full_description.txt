Jadwal (جدول, "schedule") reminds you to take each medicine at its time, and keeps reminding you until you answer. It keeps a simple record, lets you add notes, and helps you share an accurate summary with your doctor.

ALERTS YOU WON'T MISS
• At each dose time, a full-screen alert shows the medicine and dose, with Taken, Snooze and Skip.
• If you don't answer, it comes back every 10 minutes (you choose the interval) until you do.
• Works on the lock screen. You can also reply from the notification.

YOUR PLAN, YOUR WAY
• Add each medicine with its dose, time and start date.
• Dose changes over time are separate steps: increases, reductions and alternating-day patterns.
• Mark each step as entered by you, confirmed by your clinician, or still to be confirmed.

NOTES AND A REPORT FOR YOUR DOCTOR
• After each answer, add a typed or spoken note (up to 2 minutes).
• My Notes shows taken, skipped, snoozed and unlogged doses for each medicine, with a timeline.
• Share a PDF report in English or Arabic, with your voice notes in a ZIP file if you wish.
• Keep a list of questions for your doctor; they are included in the report.

MADE FOR EVERYONE
• Full Arabic interface, right to left.
• Elderly mode: larger text and buttons, one clear choice per screen.
• Short vibration and a clear "Saved ✓" after every action.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No internet permission, no account, no ads, no analytics.
• Everything stays on your phone.
• Backup and restore to a single file, optionally protected with a password, to keep a copy or move to a new phone.

Free for personal use. Business use needs approval.

Important: Jadwal is a reminder and record-keeping tool, not medical advice. It does not check doses, interactions or schedules. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions, and check with them before changing any medicine.
